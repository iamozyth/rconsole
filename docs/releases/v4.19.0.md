**rconsole 4.19.0** finds out why the server lags.

## New

- **Lag detective** (Insights → Lag detective): one click looks at tick times, loaded chunks, entities by type and the chunks they pile up in, where players are and how fast they travel, memory, CPU and VM steal time, and lists what stands out, most likely cause first.
- Each finding says where (chunk, coordinates, the nearest player) and what to do; dropped items, orbs and arrows can be removed with one click (admins).
- Runs by itself when TPS stays below the low TPS threshold, at most every 15 minutes; the last 20 reports are kept.
- The "Can't keep up" problem has a **Why?** button that opens it.

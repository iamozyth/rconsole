**rconsole 4.4.6** stops counting a harmless world generation message as an error.

## Fixed

- **"Failed to fetch mob spawner entity at (x, y, z)"** rang the problems bell as an error. Minecraft logs it while generating a dungeon whose spawner spot already holds one of the dungeon's chests (worldgen won't replace a chest), so that dungeon simply has no spawner; the chest and its loot are fine. It shows up during chunk pregeneration with Chunky. Problems now lists it as a warning, "Dungeon generated without a spawner", with that explanation, and it no longer counts toward the bell.

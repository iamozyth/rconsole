**rconsole 3.3** brings single sign-on, a profile for every player, and an editor for plugin configs. It also asks less of your server.

## New

- **Single sign-on** with authentik, Keycloak, Auth0 or any OpenID Connect provider:
  - **Login page:** shows **Continue with authentik**. Your provider handles passwords, passkeys and MFA.
  - **Roles:** come from the person's groups (`rconsole-admins`, `rconsole-moderators`, `rconsole-viewers`) at every sign-in. Anyone outside those groups is refused.
  - **Accounts:** an SSO account is tied to the provider's user ID. It has no password and never takes over a local account of the same name.
  - **Local accounts:** stay as a fallback for when the provider is down, or switch them off with `LOCAL_LOGIN=off`.
  - **Sign out** also ends the provider session.
  - **Setup:** see "Single sign-on (authentik)" in the README.
- **Player profiles:** click a player to open one.
  - **Live** while it's open: position, health, food, level, game mode, dimension, and equipment, hotbar and inventory with enchantments.
  - **Statistics** and advancements from the world files.
  - **Actions:** teleport, set game mode, heal, kick and clear inventory.
- **Config editor** (Server → Configs, admins):
  - edit plugin and mod configs in `plugins/` or `config/`
  - YAML and JSON are checked as you type
  - a diff before saving, and one-click revert

## Better

- **Lighter on the server:**
  - **RCON:** with the dashboard open, rconsole sends about 10 commands a minute instead of about 50, and a tab in the background sends none.
  - **Priority:** its systemd units give Minecraft priority for CPU and disk. rconsole, backups and downloads only take what the server leaves.
- **Minecraft 26.x:**
  - **Player files:** rconsole reads them in their new place (`world/players/…`).
  - **Shortened replies:** it copes with Paper 26.3's shortened `data get` replies.
- **RCON replies** longer than 4 KB no longer garble characters at the packet boundary.

## Upgrade

Update from Server → Updates. The update also installs the new systemd settings.

To turn on single sign-on:
1. Create the provider, application and groups in authentik.
2. Add `PUBLIC_URL` and the `OIDC_*` settings to `/etc/rconsole.env`.
3. Restart rconsole.

Until then, nothing changes.

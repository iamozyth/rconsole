**rconsole 4.18.0** lets a quiet server sleep and wakes it when a player joins.

## New

- **Sleep when empty** (Server → Overview): after 5 minutes to 2 hours without players, rconsole stops the server and answers on its port in its place.
- **Wake on join**: the multiplayer list shows the server as asleep; when one of its players joins, it starts and they join again a minute later. Choose whether only its players (whitelist, or who played there) or anyone can wake it.
- Works for every server rconsole manages; servers behind a proxy wake through the proxy.
- **Asleep** shows on the dashboard, the power card, the header and the server list, with who woke it last.

## Changed

- Start, Restart and scheduled restarts let go of a sleeping server's port before starting it; a start from outside rconsole is noticed within seconds.
- No "server went down" notification for a server that went to sleep.

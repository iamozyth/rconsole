**rconsole 3.2** is for running a server with other people: give your team their own accounts, talk to players from the browser, and bring alerts and chat into Discord.

## New

- **Roles:**
  - **Kinds of account:**
    - `admin`: everything
    - `moderator`: players, chat and moderation commands
    - `viewer`: read-only
  - **Enforcement:** the server checks the role on every request; the interface hides what a role can't do.
  - **Commands:** `node cli.js user add <name> --role moderator` and `node cli.js user role <name> viewer`. Existing accounts stay admins.
- **Web chat** (Players → Chat):
  - follow chat, `/say`, `/me`, joins and leaves live
  - write to everyone as **[Web] you**, or whisper to one player
  - click a name to whisper; kick from a message
- **Discord:**
  - **Alerts:** the same alerts as ntfy, as embeds coloured by priority, with or without ntfy.
  - **Chat relay:** a second webhook mirrors chat, joins and leaves into a channel, batched within Discord's rate limits.
  - **No pings:** mentions are switched off.
- **Audit:** the audit log notes the role of non-admin accounts.

## Upgrade

Update from Server → Updates. Nothing to configure: all existing accounts are admins, and Discord stays off until you paste a webhook.

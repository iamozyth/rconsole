**rconsole 4.15.0** puts a griefed part of the world back from a backup, without rolling back anything else.

## New

- **World → Restore area** (moderators compare, admins restore): pick a backup, a dimension and a centre (or a player's name) and compare up to 512 × 512 blocks with the backup, chunk by chunk.
- **A map of what changed**: changed blocks in red (darker means more), changed chests and signs in orange, chunks missing now striped, chunks generated since in violet. What changes by itself (leaves, flowing water and lava, grass, crops, snow, spawners, vaults) doesn't count.
- **Restore only what changed**: the clearly changed chunks are chosen for you; click or drag over the map to add or take out chunks. The server stops with a countdown, the chunks (blocks, chest contents and, if you like, mobs, item frames and armor stands) are written back and the server starts again.
- **Undo**: the replaced region files are kept for the three newest restores, and Undo puts them back.
- **Area around them** in a player's Time machine tab opens the comparison centred on that player.

## Under the hood

- New helper job `area-restore`; chunks are copied as the bytes they are, never recompressed. Audited as "area restore".
